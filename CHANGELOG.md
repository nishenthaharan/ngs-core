## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-20

### Added

- Streaming FASTQ and FASTQ.GZ parsing.
- Structural and sequence/quality-length validation.
- Paired-end identifier and record-count synchronization checks.
- JSON validation summaries and typed Python iterators.
- Synthetic fixtures, automated tests, CI, packaging, and container support.
